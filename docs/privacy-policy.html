Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Short version: this website counts visits without cookies or personal data. The qikly tool runs on your machine and collects nothing for itself.

This website (qikly.com)

  • Visit counts only. The site uses GoatCounter to count page views and clicks on its links. GoatCounter sets no cookies, collects no personal data, and does not track you across other sites.
  • Nothing else. There are no accounts, no forms, no ads and no other trackers.
  • Links out. Links to GitHub, PyPI, Towards Data Science, YouTube, X, Substack and LinkedIn take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

The qikly tool

  • Runs on your machine. There is no qikly server, no account and no telemetry.
  • Scoring your tests (qikly --score) runs entirely on your machine and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Writing tests sends your task file and the code under test to the AI model provider you choose (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic), with your own API key, straight from your machine. Nothing is sent to us.
  • A version check reads public release indexes (PyPI and GitHub) at startup, carrying no data about you or your project. You can turn it off.

The full details, including how to turn the version check off, are in the tool's privacy policy on GitHub.
